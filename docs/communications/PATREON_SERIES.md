## Voice and disclosure

- Lead with the concrete result or question.
- Show successful and failed output together.
- Explain what changed in the architecture because of the result.
- State what is implemented and what remains hypothetical.
- Explain how Patreon support converts driving hours into engineering time without
  implying that support purchases control over the invention.
