# Document semantic enrichment

## Purpose

A document source is not semantically admitted merely because its bytes, Unicode compositions, Merkle-DAG structure, physicality trajectories, file metadata, or completion markers exist.

This contract defines the missing execution boundary between exact document structure and the typed semantic state consumed by Laplace cognition.

The proof states are distinct:

```text
source bytes recovered
-> canonical content / AST admitted
-> source semantics extracted
-> testimony / calculation published
-> cross-source references reconciled
-> world-admission boundary closed
-> query / answerability proven
```

No earlier state certifies a later one.

The old iteration provides a concrete counterexample: a large document corpus can be content-addressed and structurally traversable while Matchup, Walk, and Conversation continue to rely mostly on thin lexical seed relations because the document lane emits little proposition-level semantic evidence. That behavior is historical evidence only; the clean implementation is governed by the current Constitution, Invention Model, recipe/ISA contracts, and issue #132.

## Governing laws

### Content remains content

Semantic enrichment never changes canonical content identity. Equal content from different files, releases, modalities, or sources continues to converge on one canonical identity before testimony is considered.

Source, work, document, section, sentence, span, parser, language, semantic role, sense, relation, trust, time, and extraction recipe do not salt content identity. They remain typed occurrence, physicality, interpretation, testimony, calculation, or provenance state.

### Physicality remains the exact structural carrier

Order, containment, ordinal, multiplicity, recurrence, gap, span, and trajectory are calculated from admitted structure. Semantic extraction consumes those facts; it does not recreate them as source claims.

A proposition or relation derived from a source span retains the exact structural path back to that span and its enclosing occurrence hierarchy.

### Testimony is not calculation

A document statement is testimony even when it states a mathematically or physically correct fact.

A deterministic parser, unit algebra, equation evaluator, formal calculus, or other admitted operator may independently calculate a result under an explicit scope. The source statement and the calculated result remain separate evidence kinds and may support, contradict, or contextualize one another.

### Relation type and direction are load-bearing

Semantic enrichment may emit only typed relations justified by the source profile, grammar/recipe, admitted calculus, or exact structural law. It may not replace definition, equivalence, taxonomy, part, causal, temporal, role, quantity, unit, reference, negation, modality, or other distinctions with one `RELATED_TO`, similarity score, adjacency plane, or co-occurrence edge.

A general semantic path may traverse several relation families. A taxonomy path may traverse only transitions whose declared composition law permits taxonomy. Sparse evidence never licenses relation-type erasure.

### Sense and referential identity remain explicit

Equal surface strings produce candidates, not semantic identity.

For example, the unit `ohm` and the person Georg Ohm must remain independently addressable senses/referents. Context may select, rank, or relate them; it may not merge them because their rendered surface overlaps.

## Semantic-enrichment program

A versioned enrichment recipe consumes a closed structural/evidence boundary and produces candidate typed state through the common vector-first ISA and framework lifecycle.

Its input boundary binds at least:

```text
source profile + release + artifact graph
canonical AST/content identities
physicality / occurrence trajectories
language / grammar / parser identities
source/work/document/section/span scope
valid and observation time
recipe + calculus versions
evidence / dependence epoch
authority and resource boundary
```

The program may recover, when justified by the selected source/profile:

- entry, article, section, paragraph, sentence, clause, field, and span roles;
- lexical sense and referent candidates in exact local context;
- predicate/argument and semantic-role structure;
- definitions and definitional targets;
- equivalence, taxonomy, part/whole, causal, temporal, procedural, and reference relations;
- examples, quotations, citations, cross-references, and source-defined links;
- negation, modality, quantification, qualification, uncertainty, historical scope, and contradiction markers;
- quantities, units, dimensional expressions, equations, operators, and calculus bindings;
- source-specific academic fields that cannot be recovered from generic prose alone;
- explicit ambiguous, unresolved, unsupported, rejected, and loss dispositions.

The generic engine owns execution, batching, state, evidence, lineage, publication, cancellation, replay, and receipts. Source-specific profiles provide declarative mappings and only irreducible accepted kernels. A private semantic engine per source is non-conforming.

## Starting source profiles

The configured starting estate requires distinct semantic deltas rather than one undifferentiated text parser.

### Webster 1913

Recover at minimum headword, sense, part of speech, definition, example/quotation where present, cross-reference, qualification, and exact source span. Multiple senses under one surface remain distinct.

### Britannica 1911

Recover article/headword identity, sections/subsections, definitional and explanatory propositions, references/citations, qualifications, historical applicability, quantities/equations where present, and exact article/span provenance.

### Roget

Recover thesaurus class/category structure, headwords, grouped related terms, and explicit synonymy only where the source actually warrants synonymy. Membership in one thesaurus class is not automatically an `IS_SYNONYM_OF` claim.

### Generic scientific and prose documents

Recover sentence/clause/predicate structure, quantities/units/equations, references, negation/modality/qualification, and proposition candidates through reusable language/grammar recipes. Generic prose extraction may reuse WordNet, FrameNet, VerbNet, PropBank, UD, CILI, OMW, or other admitted sources as interpretation/join evidence; none becomes universal truth or a private reasoning engine.

## Derived publication and replay

Document-derived semantic materializations are versioned derived state over immutable content/occurrence/testimony roots.

Removing or evicting an enrichment generation must not remove canonical content, AST, physicality, source occurrences, or original source testimony. Re-deriving against the identical closed boundary and recipe/calculus versions must reproduce the same logical results and receipts.

Dependent copies, mirrors, shared upstream sources, model descendants, and self-generated restatements collapse through dependence roots before independent-support calculations.

## Coverage and amplification receipt

Every selected source/profile publishes a receipt with explicit denominators and dispositions. At minimum:

```text
bytes / concrete nodes / AST nodes / physicality vertices
entries / articles / sections / sentences / clauses / spans
sense/referent candidates: resolved / ambiguous / unresolved
predicates / arguments / semantic roles
relations by family and direction
negation / modality / qualification structures
quantities / units / dimensional expressions / equations / operators
citations / references / cross-profile joins
source testimony roots
independent calculations / derivations
rejected / unsupported / loss by reason
query-reachable semantic endpoints
candidate / published derived cells
```

A large entity, trajectory, byte, or row count with near-zero semantic reachability fails semantic-profile completion. It may still prove successful structural admission, but it cannot be reported as a successful knowledge boundary.

## Held-out corpus-to-knowledge gate

The electrical corpus provides a compact falsifier because the selected estate already contains dictionaries, encyclopedia material, and scientific/electrical texts.

The test target is not a hard-coded answer. The source documents remain held-out evidence and the semantic result must arise from the generic/source-profile program.

After the relevant document profiles are admitted:

- `volt` and `ohm` expose short typed paths through electrical potential/current/resistance/unit/calculus structure rather than only generic `unit -> quantity -> entity` ancestry;
- source spans containing statements or equations equivalent to `V = I R` are reconstructable and attributable;
- when the numeric/unit calculus is available, the relationship is independently calculable under its exact scope while source testimony remains separately visible;
- `ohm` the unit and Georg Ohm the person remain distinct referential candidates;
- evicting the document-semantic generation removes the additional derived semantic evidence while preserving canonical document structure and source occurrences;
- identical re-derivation reproduces the same result/receipt;
- Matchup, Connection/A-star, Conversation, and other public consumers read the same typed state rather than feature-private shortcuts.

Additional held-out fixtures must include at least one Webster entry, one Britannica article, one ordinary scientific/prose passage, and one polysemous surface.

## Frayed-edge relationship

Frayed-edge discovery is downstream of adequate source semantics, not a substitute for them.

A missing relation is a meaningful constrained vacancy only after the selected boundary proves that the relevant structure, source semantics, and admissible relation families were actually available to the program. Failure to extract propositions from an admitted document is an extraction/profile deficit, not automatically a Gödel/frayed-edge discovery event.

Therefore the receipt for a frayed-edge candidate must distinguish at least:

- source semantics not extracted;
- unresolved sense/referent;
- inaccessible or unadmitted source boundary;
- admissible relation absent inside a closed boundary;
- contradictory relation present;
- structural motif predicts a missing role;
- resource/search exhaustion.

Only the appropriate typed state may activate the corresponding discovery program.

## Deliberate defects

Acceptance must reject implementations that:

- admit every document byte/AST node but emit no semantic evidence;
- replace typed relations with co-occurrence or n-gram edges;
- replace relation families with one generic similarity/adjacency score;
- equate a surface string with one permanent sense;
- drop source span/context/provenance/dependence lineage;
- turn a deterministic equation result into source testimony;
- count mirrors/copies as independent corroboration;
- use an opaque external answer-writing model to invent edges without an exact declared extraction recipe and source-span receipt;
- route Conversation directly to raw containers and call that semantic extraction;
- hard-code `volt`, `ohm`, `V=IR`, or any held-out expected fact;
- report non-empty prose, non-empty rows, or a successful structural seed as semantic-profile completion.
