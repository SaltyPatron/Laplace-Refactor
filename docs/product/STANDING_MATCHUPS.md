# Typed standing, matchups, onboarding, and realization

Status: required product architecture.

This document elaborates the evidence, trust, cognition, and realization laws in
`CONSTITUTION.md` and `INVENTION_MODEL.md`. It is derived from inventor-direct
correction that Glicko-2 exists to compare real typed participants under observed
outcomes, not to turn defaults into permanent flat scores. If this document conflicts
with either higher authority, execution fails closed to the higher authority.

## 1. Standing is not identity, truth, importance, or traversal

A standing state is an immutable, queryable result of a declared rating program over a
closed observation boundary. It never enters content identity and never rewrites an
entity, physicality, occurrence, testimony record, relation law, source assertion, or
prior standing epoch.

Glicko-2 contributes three useful state dimensions when its match model is applicable:

- rating: current estimated standing in one declared arena;
- rating deviation: uncertainty in that estimate;
- volatility: observed instability of that standing over rating periods.

Those values do not independently mean truth. They do not define a relation, decide
which transitions are legal, replace dependence analysis, perform traversal, or create
a universal relevance order. A cognition or realization program may consume a
standing state as one typed input after it has selected the applicable arena, relation
law, context, time, evidence epoch, and completion obligation.

Formal calculation, exact structural facts, testimony, and earned standing remain
separate. A replayable proof of `2 + 2 = 4` is not decided by a popularity matchup.
A source assertion that `2 + 2 = 5` remains an exact witnessed assertion while the
proposition is contradicted by the active arithmetic calculus.

## 2. Every standing belongs to a typed arena

There is no entity-wide scalar called importance or trust. A standing coordinate is
identified by the complete declared scope required to interpret its matches, including
at least:

```text
rated participant identity and participant role
matchup/arena kind
relation or evaluation law
world, context, language, modality, and valid-time scope as applicable
evidence and dependence boundary
rating recipe and version
standing epoch
```

The same canonical entity may therefore have different standings as a source, parser,
relation candidate, referent, firmware operation, tool route, lexical realization,
translation, or prediction. A standing earned in one arena cannot silently seed,
replace, or rank another arena.

Examples of legitimate typed arenas include:

- a source's reliability for a declared relation family and domain;
- competing propositions under an adjudication program;
- a parser or decomposer interpretation against exact reconstruction and held-out
  structure;
- a firmware operation or tool route against task completion and observed
  consequences;
- a candidate referent against discourse and world constraints;
- a language realization candidate against the completed semantic act, grammar,
  register, orthography, and exact reconstruction obligations.

A noun, function word, punctuation mark, code token, pixel, or tensor axis is not
globally more important than another constituent. Its relevance is calculated inside
the active program. A noun may dominate entity anchoring while a particle, negation,
comma, bracket, or semicolon dominates grammar, polarity, scope, exact reconstruction,
or executable code.

## 3. Defaults are first-state priors, not permanent trust

Each accepted arena declares a versioned default state for each admissible participant
class. The contract may select different initial rating, deviation, and volatility for
source classes, relation classes, grammars, tools, firmware operations, or other typed
roles, but the values and rationale must be explicit and receipted. This document does
not invent those numbers.

`New X onboarding` has one exact meaning:

1. the first eligible observation resolves canonical X and the applicable standing
   coordinate;
2. if no prior state exists for that exact coordinate, the engine creates an immutable
   zero-match state using the coordinate's declared defaults;
3. the first admissible matchup updates from that default state;
4. every later rating period starts from the immediately preceding published state for
   that coordinate;
5. replay of the same admitted matchup boundary returns the same state and receipt and
   cannot count the match twice.

Defaults are used once per standing coordinate. They are not reapplied on every fold,
source ingest, process restart, query, or new occurrence. A known entity entering a new
arena receives that arena's default because the standing coordinate is new; its earned
standing in existing arenas remains unchanged.

Source type is consequently a prior and a separately queryable trust dimension, not a
permanent source verdict. A newly observed academic dataset, military report, social
account, photograph, user photograph, or user prompt may begin from a declared typed
prior. The particular source, sensor, account, report series, curator, parser, and
relation families must then earn their own standings from attributable outcomes.

## 4. A matchup is a witnessed comparison, not co-occurrence

A matchup exists only when a declared recipe can identify:

```text
participant and opponent
arena and participant roles
pre-match standing states
observed or reproducibly calculated outcome
outcome kind and score mapping
evidence roots and dependence closure
context and valid time
rating period and ordering law
program, parameters, engine, and receipt
```

Raw adjacency, frequency, co-occurrence, parser output, source reputation, or a returned
row does not create a match. The outcome must test the proposition or operation the
match claims to evaluate.

The opponent is real. Once an opponent has standing in the arena, its prior rating and
deviation participate in the update. A neutral default is valid only for a participant
whose exact standing coordinate has never been rated. Replaying every event against a
fixed neutral opponent reduces Glicko-2 to a saturating count and is a deliberate
defect.

Outcomes remain typed before any Glicko score mapping. Confirmation, refutation, draw,
uncertain, absent, unknown, denied, exhausted, and invalid execution are not aliases.
Only outcomes admitted by the arena recipe become rating results. Unknown is not a
loss, missing evidence is not refutation, and an implementation fault is not an
epistemic result.

## 5. Proposition and source updates require a return leg

When sources testify about a proposition, adjudication may use their prior typed
standings while calculating the proposition's next standing. That cannot be the only
leg.

After a later independent observation, formal calculation, prediction outcome, or
completed adjudication boundary determines how the proposition performed, each
eligible asserting source can participate in a return-leg matchup against that outcome.
A corroborated source can gain standing; a refuted source can lose standing; uncertainty
can remain unresolved under the declared outcome law. The source's next state then
becomes the prior state used in later periods.

The return leg is epoch-delayed and dependence-aware. A source cannot use the consensus
it helped create as independent evidence of its own reliability in the same epoch.
Copies, mirrors, quotations, shared datasets, model descendants, and derived claims
collapse to their eligible dependence roots before matchup cardinality is calculated.
One thousand descendants of one observation cannot manufacture one thousand games.

The exact participant/opponent orientation belongs to the arena recipe. A relation
cell, source, proposition, parser, route, or realizer can be a rated participant only
when the recipe states what counts as its opponent and what observed outcome evaluates
it.

## 6. Rating periods, publication, and replay

Match events are immutable. Rating states are derived immutable epochs.

A rating program declares:

- the closed input event boundary;
- deterministic grouping into rating periods;
- order, tie, inactivity, deviation, volatility, and numeric rules;
- prior-state identity;
- dependence-root reduction;
- complete and partial publication behavior;
- exact state and receipt serialization.

Processing order cannot change a result for the same event boundary. A later event
publishes a successor state; it never modifies the earlier state. Historical queries
select an as-of standing epoch. Current queries select the latest completely published
eligible epoch.

No rating state is published when a required participant, opponent, outcome, prior
state, dependence closure, or numeric operation is missing. The machine returns a typed
why-not state instead of substituting a default or flat score.

## 7. Language competence and rendering are earned capabilities

`Laplace speaks language L` is not one flag and is not established because text in L
was ingested or reconstructed. It is a query over witnessed and calculated capabilities
such as:

```text
script and language identification
exact concrete-syntax recovery
morphology, grammar, roles, and discourse interpretation
concept and referent resolution
semantic-act completion
realization in language, locale, register, orthography, and modality
exact or declared-loss recomposition
multi-turn correction and consequence handling
```

A completed semantic act precedes rendering. The realizer then constructs exact Unicode
or another requested modality under a language/realization recipe. Nouns, function
words, particles, punctuation, whitespace, morphology, word order, and prosody compete
only in the typed realization obligations where they have a role. Successful semantic
preservation, grammaticality, reconstruction, user correction, independent review,
and observed downstream consequences can produce match outcomes for the responsible
realizer, grammar, operator, or firmware route.

A successful rendering must not award one undifferentiated win to every constituent or
every explanation emitted in the same event. Evidence is assigned only to the exact
operation and distinction the observed outcome tested.

## 8. Required acceptance and deliberate defects

Implementation is not complete until native, PostgreSQL, SQL, and managed routes prove:

- a new participant receives its arena's exact default state once;
- the first matchup updates from that default;
- a second matchup consumes the first published result rather than the default;
- replay is idempotent and duplicate events cannot add games;
- a known entity entering a new arena initializes only that new coordinate;
- source-type defaults remain distinguishable from earned source standing;
- a source receives return-leg wins, draws, losses, or unresolved outcomes from later
  admissible evidence without self-corroboration;
- opponent rating and deviation come from the opponent's prior state;
- independent roots and copied descendants produce different matchup cardinalities;
- context, relation family, language, modality, and valid-time scopes cannot leak
  standing into one another;
- formal derivation and exact physicality facts bypass popularity voting while retaining
  provenance and receipts;
- query ranking can use standing without writing a permanent global importance score;
- language realization preserves semantically decisive function words, punctuation,
  particles, whitespace, and code syntax;
- every state and matchup replays as-of its immutable epoch.

Deliberate defects must reject default reset on every fold, fixed-neutral opponent,
flat source-trust constants as earned standing, raw witness-count games, copied-evidence
amplification, cross-arena score reuse, ingest-order dependence, unknown-as-loss,
absence-as-refutation, whole-event success propagation, and noun-over-function-word or
punctuation global ranking.

Historical old-iteration issues `SaltyPatron/Laplace#1303` and
`SaltyPatron/Laplace#1321` are retained only as counterexamples: source trust was
asserted rather than earned, and the opponent rating was held at the neutral default on
every fold. The clean implementation must prove those defects impossible rather than
port their schema or code.
