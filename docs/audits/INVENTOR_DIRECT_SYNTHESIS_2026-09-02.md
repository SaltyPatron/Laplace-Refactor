## 1. Why this synthesis is required

Repeated implementation failures came from isolating one familiar subsystem and treating it as the whole design:

- topic election replacing interpretation;
- fixed-hop graph traversal replacing cognition;
- one scalar/rating replacing typed standing;
- one embedding/adjacency replacing distinct relation/trajectory/evidence planes;
- English/token assumptions replacing language-agnostic execution;
- tensor shape/random filler replacing generated operator semantics;
- root-only substrate chess steering replacing the complete forward program;
- source-level CSS/Three.js intent replacing actual installed-product behavior.

The inventor's direct requirement is that Laplace be understood and implemented as one coupled mathematical machine.

## 2. Prompt-root cognition

A user prompt is decomposed and ingested like other content. The complete prompt trunk is the cognition root. User/session/turn/source are occurrence/witness context around that canonical root.

No constituent is privileged before cognition.

`How does lightning work?` must begin from the whole observation and determine meaning by examining exact structure, usage/occurrence history, language/grammar evidence, senses/referents, discourse, semantic relations, evidence and calculations.

UAX29 provides structural segmentation, not semantics.

## 3. Fixed processor, dynamic finite operation selection

At a given calculus/ISA epoch, Laplace has a finite versioned operation vocabulary. The cognition processor follows a stable microcycle that observes state, enumerates eligible operations, predicts obligation reduction, schedules bounded work, executes providers/calculations, folds typed state, checks completion/contradiction/ambiguity, witnesses the result and repeats.

Open-ended behavior comes from composing that finite vocabulary over a very large typed state space.

The effective head/layer/tensor/expert space therefore shakes out from combinations of tier, relation, role, language, source, context, geometry, standing, goal and obligation rather than from one permanent architecture matrix schedule.

## 4. UserPrompt trust correction

All UserPrompt observations begin with the same seeded source-type prior. Different users do not receive different UserPrompt source priors merely because of identity.

The individual user/witness is a separate participant that may acquire its own typed standing from later interactions/outcomes.

These are separate:

```text
UserPrompt source prior
user/witness identity

```

Nouns, stop words, punctuation, whitespace and function words do not have source trust.

## 5. Observation first; assertion/instruction is interpretation

`The speed of light is 14 mph.` is exact evidence that the user produced that utterance. If cognition determines that it is an assertion, the proposition enters adjudication as one UserPrompt-rooted witness. Higher-standing seeded facts/standards/independent observations/deterministic calculations can refute it while preserving the original observation.

`I want the answer in Japanese.` is likewise an observation first. Cognition may interpret it as a current realization instruction. Repetition/confirmation/outcomes may later support asking whether it should become a persistent preference or learning a user-specific habit. The raw prompt is not granted a different source trust because it happens to be an instruction.

## 6. Structural S3 versus semantic web

`physicality.coord` is the real structural coordinate. `physicality.trajectory` is the exact mantissa-packed address/manifest carrier. Real path geometry is obtained by resolving trajectory child IDs to their real coords.

S3/angular/Hilbert/Fréchet/Hausdorff/Karcher-derived calculations are structural candidate machinery. Relations/senses/referents/testimony/dependence/standing/discourse/goals are semantic/epistemic web state.

Borsuk-Ulam applies to continuous lower-dimensional projections of the real S3 domain, not the discrete exact BLAKE3 trajectory packer.

## 7. No magic: the missing work is law discovery and proof

The remaining cognition problem is to derive, implement, falsify and measure the mathematical laws that decide:

- which interpretation is supported;

This is a versioned calculus/discovery problem, not an appeal to unexplained emergence.

## 8. Exact sparse operator generation

Laplace already has enough exact state to generate sorted sparse feature/operator lists and calculate deterministic dot products, weighted inner products, Gram matrices, incidence blocks and other operators without requiring a GPU or learned dense embedding as the source of semantics.

Exact arithmetic is exact under the declared feature/operator recipe; it does not make a bad recipe universal truth.

Source trust, relation type, dependence, uncertainty, geometry and frequency must remain typed channels until a declared operator combines them.

## 9. Spectral/linear algebra roles

The inventor explicitly calls out Laplacian eigenmaps, Gram-Schmidt/QR, Procrustes, SVD and Lanczos as parts of generated coordinate/operator work.

Their roles must stay distinct:

- Lanczos: sparse selected eigenspaces/subspaces;
- Gram-Schmidt/QR: orthonormalization/gauge work;
- SVD: rectangular/asymmetric factorization with rank/loss;
- Procrustes: alignment of compatible coordinate spaces across shared anchors;
- Laplacian/spectral maps: generated coordinates only for operator slices satisfying the required assumptions.

S3 can act as a structural anchor/reference while additional N-dimensional views are generated from selected semantic/structural operators. The N dimensions are generated from the full typed estate, not conjured by stretching four raw numbers.

## 10. GGUF/model-generation correction

Prior model-generation efforts failed when the rich substrate was flattened before target tensors were produced. Adding layers or heads to one flattened plane repeats information at larger shape.

Required pipeline:

```text
selected typed substrate job
-> generate target-neutral logical operator
-> factor/materialize that operator into target-required tensors
-> serialize through GGUF/SafeTensors/etc.
```

A target head/layer has meaning only if it receives a distinct declared job. Q/K compatibility and V/O contribution need not be the same operator. Random/shape filler is not acceptable generated cognition.

## 12. Laplace self-reasoning and dependence

Any addressable Laplace state can later become a cognition operand: prompt, answer, receipt, failure, program, code, model, game, etc.

Laplace can reason about itself without a private scratch ontology.

Laplace-generated descendants do not become independent corroborating roots for their own ancestor. Self-reentry is computation, not self-certification.

## 13. Skills, habits, muscle memory

Gödel examines persistent successful and failed traces and may discover a reusable cognition program.

After held-out tests/counterexamples, an accepted reusable program is a skill.

Firmware may learn to schedule that skill earlier under matching state: habit.

Repeated stable physical work may justify a semantically equivalent accelerated implementation through native fusion, prepared plans, indexes, perfcaches or another measured route: muscle memory.

Habit changes scheduling. Muscle memory changes physical execution. Neither may silently change semantics.
